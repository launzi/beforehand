Useful next steps from patterns on your device.

Your history stays here

This feature reads the last 90 days of Chrome history on this device when you press Scan. It uses website addresses, visit times and product-page titles to find repeated searches, new setup activity, repeated visits, and activity that went quiet. It does not read page content, cookies, downloads, or other profiles.

Raw history is processed in memory and is not uploaded or saved by this extension. After you review a request and press Run, a minimal signal (website hostname or scrubbed search term, day-level dates, and counts) goes to Nebius Token Factory for an AI suggestion. Optional research sends a scrubbed query to Tavily and selected search results to Nebius.

Scrubbing cannot detect every personal detail. Read every request before sending. Your API keys stay in local extension storage and are sent only to their respective provider for authentication. No sale, ads, or analytics.

A local audit log retains up to 500 records for 30 days, including the scrubbed requests you approved. You can export or delete it in Settings. The free extension uses your own provider accounts; provider charges may apply.

Korea quote requests send only the scrubbed product name and your destination country to Korea Concierge. Quotes create nothing and charge nothing. Safe quote amounts and product names may then go to Nebius for interpretation; product URLs and visit times do not go to Korea Concierge.

Korea Concierge is operated by the developer of this extension (Munteok). Quotes are free; a service fee is included only if you choose to buy through it.

Until you agree, no history is read and no provider is contacted. Consent is saved locally with this disclosure version. Updated disclosures require agreement again. Withdraw consent below to delete keys, trace and consent.
