What beforehand does
On supported shopping sites, beforehand reads the product on the page you are viewing and checks whether you can buy the same item from Korea, delivered, and what it costs.

What leaves your device
Only when auto-check is on, or when you click "Check Korea price":
• the product name and brand shown on the page
• your delivery country
They go to Korea Concierge (concierge.youngji.kim), run by Munteok, the developer of this extension.

What never leaves your device
Your browsing history, the page address, the price on the page, other tabs and your identity. beforehand has no access to your browsing history.

What Korea Concierge does with it
It uses the product name, brand and delivery country to find the item, or Korean alternatives, and to quote a delivered price. It keeps a demand record with a normalized product name, a category, the delivery country, the result type, a channel label and your browser family. It does not keep your IP address with that record. To limit abuse it counts requests per 10 minutes under a keyed hash of your IP address; those counters are deleted within 24 hours.

If you buy
"Buy from Korea" opens a checkout page in a new tab. Your email and name go to Korea Concierge to create the order. You then pay on Stripe (card) or PayPal; if you pay by card, Stripe also receives your email and name. beforehand does not store them and never sees your card. The seller is Munteok. Import duty and tax are estimated and paid on delivery. If the item is out of stock in Korea, you get a full refund.

Your controls
Turn checks off for one site, or turn automatic checks off, from the toolbar button. Settings lets you export or delete the local log of checks (up to 500, kept 30 days). "Turn off and delete my data" removes all local data.
