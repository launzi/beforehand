MUNTEOK

Settings

Keys and policy are stored only in chrome.storage.local on this device.

Provider keys

Saving does not contact either provider. Keys are not encrypted by this extension. They are never included in traces.

Without a Tavily key, research briefings are denied. Other skills can still run.

Search mandate

Hold blocks research. To confirm, change this setting to Allow, save, scan again, then review and Run. A Run click cannot override a held or denied mandate.

Each hostname also excludes its subdomains from signals and search-term extraction. Existing prepared requests are invalidated when settings change.

Local data

History records are not saved. The audit log keeps at most 500 records for 30 days; expired records are removed when the extension is next opened or used. Deletion here does not delete Chrome history or provider-side records.
